📄 Paper Folding Activity – Learn Powers of 2

A fun visual activity for Class 8 students to understand exponents using simple paper folds.

🎯 Learning Objective

Students discover how repeated doubling creates powers of 2.

  • 1 fold = 2 parts = 2¹
  • 2 folds = 4 parts = 2²
  • 3 folds = 8 parts = 2³
  • 4 folds = 16 parts = 2⁴

🧰 Materials Needed

📄 1 Sheet Paper
✏️ Pen / Marker
👩‍🏫 Curious Students
🧠 Prediction Skills

👀 Visual Demonstration

Fold 1 Time

2 Parts = 2¹

Fold 2 Times

4 Parts = 2²

Fold 3 Times

8 Parts = 2³

Fold 4 Times

16 Parts = 2⁴ (Imagine smaller sections)

🤔 Ask Students

  • If we fold 6 times, how many parts? 64
  • If we fold 10 times, how many parts? 1024
  • Why are numbers growing so fast?

🧑‍🏫 Teacher Script

“Every fold doubles the number of parts. Doubling again and again is called a power of 2.”

“So instead of writing 2 × 2 × 2, we write 2³.”

💡 Extension Ideas

Use chocolates, coins, or students standing in groups to show doubling physically.
